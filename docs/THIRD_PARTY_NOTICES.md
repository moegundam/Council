Keep the Oh My Pi v18.1.2 MIT license/notices; identify optional Codex CLI 0.146.0 as Apache-2.0; use the Redis 8.2.1 upstream AGPLv3 option; preserve sharp/libvips LGPL or combined-license obligations and required source or relinking information; preserve Lightning CSS MPL-2.0 notices and corresponding source; and attribute caniuse-lite under CC-BY-4.0.
