# 社群行為準則 / Community code of conduct

## 中文版

我們致力維持友善、專業且無騷擾的合作環境。請尊重不同背景與觀點，針對技術與行為提出具體意見，避免侮辱、威脅、歧視、騷擾、未經同意揭露私人資訊或其他不安全行為。維護者可移除不當內容並限制參與。事件請依 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 所述私人管道回報。

## English version

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free collaboration space. Respect different backgrounds and viewpoints, give specific feedback about work and behavior, and avoid insults, threats, discrimination, harassment, unauthorized disclosure of private information, or other unsafe conduct. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and limit participation. Report incidents through the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
